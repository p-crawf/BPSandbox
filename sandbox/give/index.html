Join Us as We Share the Biblical Story With People Everywhere

BibleProject is a registered 501(c)3 nonprofit organization and has been fully crowdfunded from the beginning. In 2014, we started BibleProject with a conviction that if our work truly provided value, an audience would join us and help make more projects possible.
Years later, we have a thriving community of generous patrons who support us as we create videos, podcasts, classes, reading plans and more. All these resources help people worldwide experience the Bible as a unified story that leads to Jesus.

FAQ

Updates or end your recurring gifts on the website or app by signing in with the BibleProject account associated with your giving.

Website: Go to Giving on the Account page to manage recurring gifts and payment methods.
App: Go to the Me tab and find the Giving section to manage recurring gifts and payment methods.

If your gift was initially set up through an email address that isn't an active BibleProject account, you can still register it here.

Here are a few additional ways to give:

  • Set up ACH direct deposit giving with your bank account. The option to set up direct deposit giving will appear on the form above for gifts of $100+.
  • One-time and recurring gifts through PayPal.
  • Personal or BillPay checks. (See mailing address below)
  • Stock, bonds, mutual funds, or cryptocurrency. Learn more here.
  • Funding or charitable foundation opportunities. Contact our Finance team here.

You can send personal checks or BillPay gifts to:

BibleProject

Make sure your name and email address are on the check so we can link your gifts to your BibleProject account.

To access the matched giving employer eligibility search tool, set up a new one-time or recurring gift. Once your gift processes successfully, follow the post-giving steps to see the Double the Donation tool.

BibleProject accepts gifts to our general fund through several leadership-approved giving methods. Learn more about supporting our organization's mission through the standard gift acceptance policy outlined here.
