The Spirit of God, or Holy Spirit, is God’s personal life-giving energy and presence. The Spirit sustains everything and partners with humans to renew creation. Trace the theme of God’s Holy Spirit through Scripture in three parts.

The Main Idea

As God's invisible, personal presence, the Spirit of God animates and sustains all creation, infusing the world with God's active presence. The story of the Bible points us toward a humanity filled with and empowered by God’s Spirit to bring order and life.

Part 1

The Spirit in the Hebrew Bible

The Bible introduces the “Spirit of God” (Hebrew: ruakh Elohim) as God’s personal, invisible, animating presence that creates and sustains all life. God’s Spirit empowers individuals like Joseph, Bezalel, and Israel’s judges for wise leadership, creative craftsmanship, and powerful rescue. And figures like Moses and David offer previews of what Spirit-empowered leadership can look like.

Part 3

The Spirit and Us

The Holy Spirit fills Jesus’s followers on the day of Pentecost, creating the unified and diverse community of the Church. The believers receive Spirit-empowerment in the form of spiritual gifts and prophetic insights so that they can all contribute to the flourishing of the community. Ultimately, the Holy Spirit is revealed to be not just God's life-giving presence and power, but a divine person, distinct from yet unified with the Father and the Son.

Podcast Episodes
Episodes 10-12 cover the birth of the Church and the nature and purpose of spiritual gifts, focusing on the role of prophecy. Episodes 13-15 examine the unique portrayals of the Spirit in Paul's and John's writings and trace how the early Church developed a theology of the Holy Spirit from the Scriptures.
