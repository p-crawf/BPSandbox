FAQ

Once you download the app, you'll be able to explore our entire library of videos, podcasts, classes, and articles. We always promote our latest releases on the Home tab, and we recommend you start there.

The possibilities are endless! Our videos, podcasts, classes, and articles are designed to help you develop new skills to explore the entirety of the biblical text. We hope you'll grow to see how this ancient collection of literature really is a unified story that leads to Jesus.

The app is currently optimized to run on iOS (15.5+) and Android (8+) mobile devices. The app also works on most tablets but is not optimized for those devices.

The website and app feature our same library of videos, podcasts, articles, and classes. However, the app makes it easier to access our content on the go, track your progress, and engage with more study resources. The website is ideal for people who prefer viewing our content on larger screens.

At the moment, the app is only accessible in English. We have received many requests for our app in different languages and are very open to exploring this in the future.

Visit our help center to explore other questions about the BibleProject app.

Everything we make is available for free because of a generous community of patrons.
