The Exodus Way

Discover the Bible's Three-Part Rescue Story

The exodus is more than a story. It's a pattern throughout Scripture that describes a way of life. Learn how this biblical theme points us to Jesus.

The Main Idea

The exodus is a biblical pattern rooted in the story of Israel's departure from Egypt — out of enslavement, through the wilderness, and into freedom. What began in the exodus reaches its full meaning in Jesus, who leads a new exodus out of sin and death, through transformation, and into new creation.
